WHO WE ARE

Not just a legacy.
A living practice.

SHETH ASSOCIATES is a litigation and dispute-resolution practice with professional roots in Rajkot, Gujarat, tracing its legal heritage to 1958.

For more than six decades, the practice has evolved through successive generations of advocates, changing areas of law, developing procedural frameworks and an increasingly complex litigation environment.

The significance of that history lies in the knowledge accumulated through generations of courtroom work, legal drafting, dispute resolution and interaction with different branches of the justice system.

Our identity can be expressed simply: Legacy gives us perspective. Preparation gives us strength. Advocacy gives us purpose.

“A client's legal problem deserves careful understanding before a legal solution is proposed.”

OUR LITIGATION PHILOSOPHY

Preparation before
appearance.

Our practice is structured around understanding the dispute, identifying the real legal issue, analysing law, procedure and evidence, preparing the legal record and advocating before the appropriate forum.

The question is not simply whether a case can be filed. It is what legally available course of action is most appropriate in the circumstances.

01

Understand

Facts, chronology, parties, rights, obligations, documents, proceedings and the client's objective.

02

Identify

Find the real legal issue rather than assuming the apparent dispute is the entire dispute.

03

Analyse

Examine substantive law, procedural law and the evidence capable of establishing the position.

04

Strategise

Select among advice, negotiation, notice, settlement, mediation, litigation, arbitration, appeal, revision or execution.

05

Document

Turn facts into a coherent legal record through precise notices, pleadings, applications, affidavits and agreements.

06

Advocate

Transform preparation into effective representation through command over the record, law, evidence and procedure.

07

Adapt

Respond intelligently when new documents, evidence, pleadings, orders or settlement opportunities change the litigation landscape.

08

Enforce

Consider execution, recovery, possession, compliance, appeal defence and post-judgment remedies where required.

PROFESSIONAL RESPONSIBILITY
“

No false certainty. Legal advice should distinguish between what the law provides, what the evidence establishes, what can reasonably be argued and what ultimately depends upon judicial determination.

Litigation involves uncertainty. Courts decide disputes based upon law and evidence; opposing parties may contest facts; procedural developments may affect a matter; and judicial discretion may become relevant. Responsible representation therefore requires precision about both legal entitlement and practical uncertainty.
