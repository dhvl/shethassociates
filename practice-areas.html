# OUR PRACTICE AREAS

Comprehensive legal representation across multiple forums.

At SHETH ASSOCIATES, we provide comprehensive legal representation, litigation strategy, advisory services and documentation support across a broad spectrum of civil, criminal, commercial, revenue, family and regulatory disputes.

From pre-litigation advice and drafting to trial, appellate proceedings and execution, our team assists clients throughout the life cycle of a dispute.

A FULL-SPECTRUM LITIGATION PRACTICE

One dispute may require
multiple legal lenses.

Our practice areas are interconnected. A single dispute may involve multiple areas of law—for example, a property dispute may simultaneously involve civil litigation, revenue proceedings, criminal allegations and execution proceedings.

Accordingly, SHETH ASSOCIATES adopts a cross-disciplinary litigation approach, enabling the legal team to examine the dispute from multiple procedural and substantive perspectives.

Our objective is not merely to initiate proceedings, but to identify the most appropriate legal remedy, forum and strategy for achieving the client's legitimate objectives.
